✨ 4 Simple Rules So Your Hand-Drawn QR Scans Instantly

  • 1
    Draw the 3 Big Corner "Eyes" First:

    Located at Top-Left, Top-Right, and Bottom-Left. Each is a 7×7 square: outer black border, 1-square white ring inside, and a solid 3×3 black center. Phone cameras look for this exact pattern to detect the code.

  • 2
    Use Standard 5mm Graph Paper (or print our stencil):

    Standard math/grid notebook paper with 5mm squares is perfect! A 25×25 code takes only 12.5 cm × 12.5 cm (about 5×5 inches) — the perfect size for a romantic greeting card or letter.

  • 3
    Leave 1–2 White Squares (Quiet Zone) Before Drawing Your Heart:

    Draw your beautiful red or pink heart outline around the entire code, but keep at least 1 or 2 blank grid squares between the QR modules and your heart stroke. This prevents camera algorithms from confusing the heart line with QR data.

  • 4
    Use a High-Contrast Dark Pen:

    A black gel pen, dark fine-liner, or rich crimson marker works best. Fill the dark squares solidly without leaving white patches.
